Mara is preparing snack bags for a community hike. She buys 8 boxes of oat bars with 15 bars in each box. She sets aside 12 bars for the guides. She divides all remaining bars equally among 9 hiking groups. During packing, each group returns 2 bars because its bag is too full. How many bars are in each group bag after the returns, and how many returned bars are there altogether? Show the arithmetic clearly and state both answers.
